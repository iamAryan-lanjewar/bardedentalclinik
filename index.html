3D Digital Dental X-Ray

We believe getting an x-ray should feel effortless, fast, and completely safe. Our Planmeca 3D CBCT captures an ultra-clear, 360° view of your teeth, jawbone, and nerve anatomy in just 10 seconds — using up to 77% less radiation than standard medical CTs. Designed with an open-air walk-through space, there are no painful biting plates or closed tunnels. You simply stand or sit relaxed while robotic precision ensures your care is planned with gentle, pin-point accuracy.

  • 77% Ultra-Low Radiation (Patient-Safe): Certified Planmeca Ultra Low Dose™ protocols shield your body by reducing radiation exposure by up to 77% compared to conventional dental CT scans — safer than an ordinary sunny afternoon outdoors.
  • 10-Second Swift Scan (Zero Biting Pain): Open-air robotic arm executes a silent, gentle 10-second rotation around you while you breathe naturally, completely eliminating painful plastic biting blocks in your mouth.
  • 75 μm High-Definition Clarity (Zero Guesswork): Microscopic 75-micron diagnostic resolution pinpoints hidden roots, bone density, and nerve pathways to ensure your surgical implants and root canals are 100% pain-free and precise.

Comfort Dental Care

Your dental visit should feel as calming as a personal wellness retreat. We’ve designed our treatment suites around human comfort — pairing zero-gravity orthopedic memory chairs that cradle your spine with whisper-quiet digital handpieces operating below 45 decibels to eliminate drill noise anxiety. Under 5500K daylight-matched surgical illumination, every procedure is gentle, soothing, and finished with flawless cosmetic precision.

  • Zero-Gravity Orthopedic Recline: Contoured dual-density memory foam distributes body weight evenly, relieving lower back and neck pressure so you rest comfortably throughout your entire appointment.
  • Whisper-Quiet Micromotors (<45 dB): Precision electric handpieces run smoother and quieter than a library whisper, eliminating high-pitch drill noise, vibration, and dental anxiety.
  • 5500K Daylight LED Lamps (CRI 95+): Shadowless surgical lighting replicates natural midday sunlight without harsh glare, allowing Dr. Barde to craft and color-match your porcelain veneers with 100% natural harmony.
  • 99.999% Hospital-Grade Sterilization: Medical Class-B autoclave cycles and active HEPA filtered air exchange ensure every surface and touchpoint is impeccably sanitized and fresh.
